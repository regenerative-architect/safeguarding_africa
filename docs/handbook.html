Safety before action

Listen without blame, leading questions or forced retelling. Explain confidentiality limits and check local duties with qualified support. Ask what would help the child feel safer. Do not automatically contact an implicated caregiver or institution. Never confront alleged perpetrators, collect abusive images, coerce participation or expose a child publicly.

Before a referral, verify provider identity, competence, cost, hours, language, accessibility and conflicts of interest. Plan against retaliation. Stop if the next step could increase danger. A checklist or software feature cannot guarantee safety.

S01 · Immediate safety · Inference · Tier 0 · Now

Respond to a disclosure safely

Help a child reach safe, appropriate support without requiring a detailed account.

Preconditions

  • A private but accountable setting
  • A trusted adult who is not implicated
  • Locally verified referral options

Steps

  1. Listen calmly; thank the child and avoid blame or leading questions.
  2. Explain what you can keep private and any locally applicable reporting duties; do not promise secrecy.
  3. Ask what would help them feel safer now; offer choices and accessible communication.
  4. For immediate danger or injury, seek locally appropriate urgent help if safe; otherwise agree a safe referral with a trained provider.

Failure mode: Repeated retelling, an automatic parental notification or contacting an implicated official can increase harm.

Stop: Pause if a child fears retaliation, a referral is unsafe, confidentiality cannot be protected, or a proposed action involves coercion. Seek a vetted independent safeguarding professional; do not confront an alleged perpetrator.

Measure: Process: proportion of referral options whose safety and availability were checked; never count disclosures as success.

Practical and advanced guides

Start with one safe improvement

Heuristic

Choose one barrier to access and one concrete improvement. Verify a safe referral option, agree who owns the work, and set a review date. If a child is at immediate risk, use the safety pathway instead of completing programme forms.

Evidence is not the same as a suggestion

Heuristic

Evidence cards describe what a source reports. Strategy steps are an editorial synthesis, labelled Inference. Readiness thresholds and time ranges are Heuristics. Your notes, progress and measures are User-entered. None of these become verified facts when shared.

A theory of change in plain language

Heuristic

Write: if we change this procedure, then this access barrier should reduce, because of this mechanism. List assumptions and at least one plausible way it could fail. Measure the intended outcome and unintended harm separately.

Three implementation tiers, plus system reform

Heuristic

Tier 0: low-resource, non-case planning and safe information. Tier 1: coordinated referrals and local institutional changes with verified partners. Tier 2: supervised programmes, dedicated budget and safeguarding governance. Tier 3: legal, policy, evaluation or scale work with specialist oversight. Higher tier is not a better moral score.

What you can do independently

Heuristic

Improve your own policies, check public information, prepare accessible materials and fund vetted support. Decisions about a child, someone else’s data, clinical treatment, legal representation or research participation require the relevant authority, consent and professional process. “Unilateral” never means overriding rights.

Trauma-informed communication

Heuristic

Use predictable explanations, practical choices and a calm pace. Avoid blame, leading questions, forced retelling, promises of secrecy, mandatory reconciliation and public storytelling. Listen to the child’s preferences while explaining legal limits with qualified local help.

Anti-retaliation review

Heuristic

Before disclosure or action, consider who might learn of it, what access could be lost, whether the intended recipient is implicated, and what alternative support exists. Agree a safe contact method. Stop if the plan increases exposure and seek an independent professional route.

Local legal review

Heuristic

Ask a qualified adviser about the applicable jurisdiction, mandatory reporting duties, consent and assent rules, deadlines, documentation alternatives and confidentiality exceptions. This bundle provides general rights-based planning, not country-specific legal advice.

Choosing a referral partner

Heuristic

Check official identity, competence, opening hours, cost, language, accessibility, eligibility, confidentiality, reporting duties and conflicts. Agree how to know whether a referral was accepted without circulating case details. Reverify before use and keep a backup.

Budget before scale

Heuristic

Include supervision, accessibility, translation, transport, safeguarding response, maintenance and independent evaluation. Do not price only training sessions or device purchases. A service that cannot accept referrals is not ready to scale.

Interpret change carefully

Heuristic

A higher complaint count may mean more harm, better trust, improved reporting or all three. A lower count can mean fear. Compare multiple indicators, explain missingness and avoid causal claims from a simple before/after comparison.

Privacy and small numbers

Heuristic

This is a programme planner, not a case-management database. Use no names, contact details, precise locations, identity documents, allegations or child stories. Aggregate data can still identify people. Avoid small subgroups and complementary totals that allow subtraction. A count threshold alone is not anonymisation.

Safe participation

Heuristic

Participation must be voluntary, accessible, age-appropriate and possible to stop. Explain what will change and what cannot. Do not make access to services conditional on surveys, publicity or platform use. Youth should never investigate abuse or carry adult reporting duties.

Adapting across Africa

Heuristic

Start with country law, languages, rural/urban context, disability access, migration status, institutional capacity and local histories of exclusion. Evidence from one district in Uganda or South Africa is not evidence for every African country. North African and conflict-affected settings need additional local research.

Print and low bandwidth

Heuristic

Core content uses system fonts and no external images. Print the current route or the printable handbook. Preload through HTTPS or localhost to cache core assets. A file opened directly can read core content, but installation, peer tabs and storage vary by browser.

Backups and recovery

Heuristic

Export a JSON backup regularly and keep it in a protected location. Backups are plaintext. Import validates format, allowed fields, size and version, then asks you to replace local state; a pre-import snapshot is saved when storage works. Future schema versions are rejected rather than guessed.

Collaboration without hidden sharing

Heuristic

Local mode connects tabs on the same browser origin after explicit joining. Online mode downloads Trystero and contacts public signalling/ICE services. Share only a previewed catalogue-based plan snapshot. No notes, measures, acknowledgements or local research entries are shared. A recipient must choose to apply it.

Protocol and late joins

Heuristic

Peers exchange app/protocol/schema metadata automatically only after joining. Incompatible protocol/schema messages are rejected. A late join does not receive personal state automatically: a participant presses Share reviewed snapshot. Snapshots have revision and message IDs; conflicts remain explicit replacements, never silent merges.

Optional local AI

Heuristic

The deterministic guide works without a model. Optional WebLLM downloads third-party code and model files only after a button press. Inference runs locally, but download hosts see network metadata. Only fixed, non-identifying learning prompts are accepted. Generated text is unverified and cannot execute actions or change plans.

Hardware-aware choices

Heuristic

Browser device memory is approximate and often absent; it is not GPU VRAM. Use the deterministic guide on constrained or unknown hardware. If WebGPU works and there is ample memory and disk space, try the smallest offered model first. Model metadata guides selection; successful loading is the compatibility test.

Network and dependency review

Heuristic

Core use makes same-origin requests for static assets and service-worker updates. Optional model and peer modules load pinned CDN packages with their own dependencies. No analytics, geolocation, camera, microphone, push notifications or contact scanning are included. External source links navigate only when clicked.

Research ledger workflow

Heuristic

Record the question, exact source URL, publication date, study design, geography, population, finding and limitations. Distinguish full-text review from abstract or search extract. Log contradictory findings and when an entry needs review. A user-added ledger item remains unverified until a human review outside this tool.

Safe programme monitoring

Heuristic

Choose one outcome, one delivery measure and one balancing measure. Define the numerator and denominator in advance, use the same time window, note missing data and consider who is excluded. This app’s aggregate calculator is descriptive and cannot diagnose safety or compare children.

Maintenance and review

Heuristic

Nominate a content owner and safeguarding reviewer. Recheck service availability before referral, legal rules before action and technical dependencies before release. Maintain a change log. The research cutoff is 2026-09-29; no automatic research update is claimed.

Facilitator workshop: 60 minutes

Heuristic

0–10: explain boundaries and choose a fictional scenario. 10–20: map one access barrier. 20–35: select a strategy and verify assumptions. 35–45: rehearse an anti-retaliation and stop decision. 45–55: choose measures and responsibilities. 55–60: agree review and destroy unneeded notes. Never invite personal disclosures in the group.

Country adaptation worksheet

Heuristic

Record country and broad service area outside this app if sensitive. Review legal duties with counsel; confirm institutions; translate and user-test materials; budget access costs; establish independent complaints; pilot with adult programme staff; review child participation ethically; document unresolved gaps before scale.
