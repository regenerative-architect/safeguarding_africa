# Research and content provenance

Review date: 2026-09-29. Recent-work search window: 2024-09-29 through 2026-09-29. Older rights instruments and foundational trials retained deliberately. The 2024 Good School follow-up predates that window and is retained as important contrary/limiting evidence.

Searches covered violence-prevention frameworks; rights mechanisms; registration; digital harm; school inclusion; African service networks; connectivity; parenting and school trials; technical runtime documentation. Web search and Exa were used. Exa searches requested 27 results across five targeted queries; results were screened for primary authorship, relevance, design and limits. Search-result counts are not counts of included or full-text-reviewed studies. Selected publisher pages were fetched; full texts were not all reviewed. No systematic screening protocol, duplicate-independent extraction, risk-of-bias instrument or meta-analysis was performed.

## Evidence labels

Evidence: a limited paraphrase of a source finding, not proof of every application.
Inference: an editorial implementation suggestion informed by sources.
Heuristic: a practical planning rule, tier, timescale, cascade or progress measure without validation as a diagnostic scale.
User-entered: locally supplied notes, progress, measures and research records, unverified by the platform.
Unverified AI draft: optional generated explanation of a fixed public strategy, not an evidence claim.

## Traceability

data/catalog.json contains source IDs, stable source URLs, source type, publication date or explicitly unknown date, review date, scope, finding and limitations. Claims reference a source ID. Strategies list supporting background IDs. Sources link back to the strategies they inform. Infrastructure entries list source IDs and verification needs. No effect size is generated from user inputs or inferred across contexts.

## Material limitations

- Foundry HTML unavailable; full original chat unavailable. Build is based on the user specification and cached preview.
- BIRTH25 PDF fetch timed out; only the publisher/search description of the evaluation synthesis is used.
- Several living pages lack a publication date. The access date is not substituted for publication.
- CHIDIG year is inferred only from its URL path and explicitly labelled uncertain.
- GSTSCALE publication date was not established; no claim that it falls within the recent window is made.
- Direct anti-racism and persecution intervention evidence is thinner in this bank than school and caregiver violence evidence; transfer is labelled inference.
- Coverage is uneven across countries, languages and North African/conflict-affected settings. Country legal rules, helpline hours and current provider capacity were not independently verified.
- A 2026 U-Report evaluation planned for October is not reported as completed.
- Industry connectivity research has commercial framing. Registry links are not endorsements.

## Update protocol

Record a question and exact URL, verify the primary source, distinguish study design and population, extract positive and null outcomes, record limits, check dates, update claims and affected strategies together, obtain safeguarding/editorial review, increment content and cache versions, rerun tests. Never silently promote user ledger records into curated evidence.
