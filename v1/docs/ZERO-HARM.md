# Child safeguarding and misuse resistance

“Zero harm” is an operating aspiration, not a guarantee. The software cannot ensure safe real-world conduct or replace qualified services.

## Invariants built into the design

- Planning data only: no child profiles, identifiers, attachments, location capture, contact directory or allegation fields.
- No surveillance, targeting, biometric collection, suspect lists, intelligence collection or public case maps.
- No autonomous actions, auto-reporting, auto-sending, push messages, email or messaging integrations.
- No vigilantism, entrapment, coercion, forced mediation or instructions to confront alleged perpetrators.
- Every strategy contains a stop rule, failure mode and human review instruction.
- Four preparation acknowledgements gate the review message; they are not a risk score, eligibility decision or licence to act.
- Collaboration serialises only allowlisted strategy IDs and protocol metadata. Imported text is escaped, never evaluated. Incoming plans require review and reset readiness.
- Optional AI accepts only public catalogue text, renders plain text and cannot modify state or contact people.
- Gamification counts self-reported programme steps only. No reward for identifying children, disclosures, complaint totals or public exposure. It can be disabled.

## Limits and required practice

Free-text programme notes can still be misused to enter sensitive data. The software cannot reliably detect all identifying content. Users must not enter it. Local storage is not encrypted. An attacker with browser/device access can read it. Aggregation and cell suppression do not guarantee anonymity.

Before any real-world action, review the child's views, safe communication, local duties, consent/assent, confidentiality, provider competence and anti-retaliation options. A caregiver or institution may be implicated; provide an independent route. Do not promise secrecy or automatically notify someone without checking safety and applicable law.

For immediate danger, seek appropriate local emergency/health/protection help if safe. This app does not dispatch assistance and assumes no universal emergency number. Do not delay urgent support to complete checklists. Do not conduct investigations, download abusive images or collect traumatic accounts in the app.

## Threat model

Consider: coercive programme leaders, unsafe shared devices, malicious imports, hostile room participants, stale directories, misconstrued research findings, unsafe legal assumptions, AI hallucinations and dependency compromise. Mitigations reduce attack surface but do not eliminate these threats. The service registry is published context, not a vetted live referral list.

## Incident response for platform misuse

Stop sharing; disconnect the room; seek organisational safeguarding and data-protection advice; preserve only lawful minimum information in an approved system; export or delete local planning data according to policy. Do not investigate suspected abuse through this tool. Report code defects through the maintainer's chosen repository process without personal data.
